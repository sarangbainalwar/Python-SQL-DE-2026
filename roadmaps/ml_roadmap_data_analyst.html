💡 Success Tips for Your ML Journey

🎯 Start with the Basics

Don't skip the foundation. Strong math and programming skills make advanced topics easier. Focus on understanding concepts, not just code.

🏗️ Build, Build, Build

Theory is important, but practice is crucial. Build at least 2-3 projects per phase. Start simple, gradually increase complexity.

📊 Focus on Business Problems

As a data analyst pivoting to ML, emphasize business value. Frame projects around solving real business problems, not just accuracy.

🤝 Join the Community

Engage with Kaggle competitions, attend meetups, join Discord servers. Learning with others accelerates progress significantly.

📝 Document Everything

Write blog posts about what you learn. Create detailed project documentation. This solidifies learning and builds your portfolio.

⏰ Consistency Over Intensity

2-3 hours daily beats 10 hours on weekends. Make ML learning a daily habit. Progress compounds over time.
